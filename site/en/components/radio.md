# Radio

<p class="page-desc">Matches Arco Radio; radio buttons map to Arco's button type. See the <a href="https://element-plus.org/en-US/component/radio.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Element Plus docs</a> for props.</p>

## Basics & Disabled

Labels keep their color when selected; disabled items cannot be changed.

:::

## Button Groups & Sizes

The three button-group sizes match Arco small, medium and large.

::: demo radio/buttons
:::

## Standalone Sizes & Border

Standalone sizes and borders have no Arco equivalent and retain Element Plus behavior.

::: demo radio/border
:::

## Differences from Arco

- Standalone sizes and border have no Arco equivalent and keep the Element Plus form.
- Button groups keep a keyboard focus ring and different trailing spacing.
