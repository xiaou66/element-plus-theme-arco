# Popconfirm

<p class="page-desc">Matches Arco Popconfirm with small action buttons and a status icon. See the <a href="https://element-plus.org/en-US/component/popconfirm.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Element Plus docs</a> for props.</p>

## Click Confirmation

Click Delete to open the confirmation; confirm and cancel update the status.

## Differences from Arco

- The default question-mark icon differs from Arco's information icon.
- Cancel defaults to Element Plus's text style; cancel-button-type="default" gives it a neutral fill.
