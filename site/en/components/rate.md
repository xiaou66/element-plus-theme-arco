# Rate

<p class="page-desc">Matches Arco Rate with gold selected stars and themed spacing. See the <a href="https://element-plus.org/en-US/component/rate.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Element Plus docs</a> for props.</p>

## Rating & Disabled

Click stars to rate, allow-half permits half stars and disabled makes the rating read-only.
