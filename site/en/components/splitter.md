<p class="page-desc">Covers Arco Split use cases while keeping Element Plus panels and resize bars with Arco colors. See the <a href="https://element-plus.org/en-US/component/splitter.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Element Plus docs</a> for props.</p>
